| File server READ (RPC 0x80000001, function 2: fd, ptr, size, record): the IOP sends the 16-byte aligned middle by SIF DMA to ptr and the parts before and after it (up to 15 bytes each, with their addresses) in the 48-byte record; the parts are those of the size asked for, so after a short read (the end of the file) the DMA ends on the quadword after the last byte read and the part after it is not data. | ps2link (host: by ps2client) takes a negative offset of LSEEK from the current position or the end as a huge positive one: it answers with the right position (modulo 2^32), but its file is then past the end and reads give 0 bytes; PCSX2 does not. `mx_fio_lseek` seeks from the position itself. CLOSE answers with the descriptor on ps2link, 0 on PCSX2 | `tests/ps2/fio.c` on the PS2 and PCSX2 | 2026-10-24 |
| The IOP under ps2link 1.08 and under PCSX2 `-elf` runs the boot ROM's modules: `FILEIO_service` 1.01 (text 0x3fd30), `LoadModuleByEE` 1.01, MODLOAD 1.01 with 16 exports; ps2link adds SMAP, dev9, the TCP/IP stack, udptty and itself. The ROM's FILEIO is the one sbv's FILEIO patch is for (version 0x101), and maxsdk applies it (`mx_iop_patch_fileio`, on the first `mx_fio_dopen`) | `tests/ps2/iop.c` on the PS2 and PCSX2 | 2026-10-31 |
| ps2link resets the IOP each time it starts (also after a program exits) and then applies sbv's LMB and prefix-check patches itself, but not the FILEIO one: under ps2link `mx_iop_patch_lmb`/`mx_iop_patch_prefix_check` find their patch (1) and `mx_iop_patch_fileio` patches in every run; under PCSX2 `-elf` all three patch | `tests/ps2/iop.c`, ps2link `ee/ps2link.c` | 2026-10-31 |
| The IOP's memory is read with RPC RDATA from the EE (`sceSifGetOtherData`: source, destination, size in words 8–10; the answer is an RPC_END with the client in word 7) and written by SIF DMA to its address; the IOP heap is RPC 0x80000003 (function 1 allocates, 2 frees).
